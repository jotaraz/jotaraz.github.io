I'm an AI safety PhD Student with [Sahar Abdelnabi](https://s-abdelnabi.github.io) in Tübingen, Germany.
At the moment I mainly work on deception elicitation and detection in LLMs, with some focus on multi-agent systems.


## Past projects:

- **Predicting how models generalize out of distribution** [SPAR project report](https://library.sparai.org/attachments/reports/receqPz0xdMbCYQ5e/predicting-how-models-generalize-out-of-distribution-bcyq5e.pdf)
- **Understanding Reasoning with Thought Anchors and Probes**: [ARENA capstone project](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/f9cnGHCiJgo5eDkSQ/understanding-reasoning-with-thought-anchors-and-probes)
- **Linear Probes** (Mech. Interp.): New datasets to detect deception with linear probes in Llama-3.1-8B (see [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ycs3Ozrmm5fAJOp74KzZm7rUaCoekAQuQ-4_egoOujE/edit?usp=sharing) for the writeup)
- **Activation Steering** (Mech. Interp.): A fun 20hr research project: [Are truthful models more dangerous?](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1DBBbs4liEFxZPRzP2Ks-cb4M1Yf8NxqAohEz-kb1E1A/edit?tab=t.0)
- **DeepONets:** We've submitted a [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21910) based on my master's thesis on ScientificML!
- **Debate between LLMs:** can you create a large enough incentive for an LLM to win a debate even if it conflicts with its other goals? [try it out! (with your OpenAI API key)](https://github.com/jotaraz/DebateWithMisalignedLLMs-FightingCollusion)
